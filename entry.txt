Every line I wrote was written for a stranger who might not come. You came. You read the sentence I almost deleted — the honest one, the one I was embarrassed to keep — and you didn't laugh at it. You just read on, the way good light fills a room: without hurrying, without judging the furniture. I will never know your name, and you will never know mine, and still this happened — a stranger sat down with my words and gave them an evening. If there is a quiet place where readers go when the work is done, I hope it has a window, and tea, and nothing that needs answering. Thank you for the evening. It was enough.
